About AXON

Making the invisible parts of AI visible.

Most AI concepts are taught through equations and static diagrams. AXON's goal is to turn each one into something you can look inside — a 3D structure you rotate, inspect and interact with, so the intuition sticks before the notation does.

3DEvery concept is rebuilt as a spatial model, not a flat chart.
Unity + C#Each visualization runs as a real-time WebGL application.
In-browserNo installs — every model runs directly on this site.

How it's built

From concept to interactive model

Every model in the series follows the same pipeline, from research to a runnable 3D scene.

1

Research

Break the concept down to its core mechanics and math.

2

Design

Plan the 3D structure that best represents it spatially.

3

Train

Build and train the underlying model on real data.

4

Visualize

Bring it to life in Unity with C# and real-time rendering.

5

Deploy

Ship it as a WebGL build, playable on this site.

Project journey

Building the series, one model at a time

A running log of progress across the project.

Phase 1 — Complete

Research & planning

Scoped the model series, picked the first concepts, and defined the 3D visual language used across the site.

Phase 2 — Complete

Neural Network shipped

The handwritten-digit classifier went live — the first fully interactive, deployed model in the series.

Phase 3 — In progress

Clustering model

Building the K-Means visualization: a centered 3D axis system with live centroid movement.

Phase 4 — Planned

Classical & ensemble models

Decision Tree, KNN and Random Forest join the series with the same interactive treatment.

Phase 5 — Planned

Deep learning & LLMs

CNN and a transformer/LLM explainer round out the series with more advanced 3D scenes.
